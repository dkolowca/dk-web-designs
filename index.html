About Me

I'm Derek Kolowca, the person behind DK Web Designs. I build and maintain websites with an emphasis on clear information, straightforward navigation and practical features that make sites easier to manage.

My website work grew from managing real-world sites where information needs to stay current and visitors need to find what they're looking for quickly.

I've worked on school and athletic-association websites, handling page design, content updates, responsive layouts, schedules, results, standings, forms and ongoing maintenance.

I bring a practical approach to web work: understand what the site needs to accomplish, make it easy to use, and keep it manageable after launch.
